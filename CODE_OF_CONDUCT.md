# Code of conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The summary below is not a substitute for the full text.

## Our pledge

We want participation in Scopedeck to be harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experience.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission. In a security
  community this includes posting someone's unredacted reports, tokens or targets.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews) and when
someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report incidents privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/BankkRoll/scopedeck/security/advisories/new),
with "Code of conduct" in the title. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

Maintainers follow the Contributor Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines):
correction, warning, temporary ban or permanent ban, depending on the impact of the behaviour.
